title: "Instructor Notes"
---
This workshop is intended for biodiversity data managers, environmental scientists, and data officers who work with species occurrence data and want to publish it to GBIF.

## Before the Workshop

- Ensure all participants have installed [OpenRefine](https://openrefine.org/download) and [DB Browser for SQLite](https://sqlitebrowser.org/).
- Share the workshop data files in advance (see the [data folder](https://github.com/DimEvil/bio_mobilization_workshop/tree/gh-pages/data)).
- Ask participants to register a GBIF account at <https://www.gbif.org/user/profile> before the first day.

## Tips for Instructors

- The OpenRefine exercises work best when participants use their own datasets. Encourage them to bring a real dataset to clean.
- The IPT sessions require access to a test IPT instance. Contact the Belgian Biodiversity Platform if you need credentials.
- Allow extra time for the Darwin Core alignment discussions — participants often have many questions about term mapping.
